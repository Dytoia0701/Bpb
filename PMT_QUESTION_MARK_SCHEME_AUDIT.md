# PMT question and mark-scheme reference pass — Bridge Protocol v19

## Sources inspected

This is a first reference pass, not a claim that every PMT PDF across every subject has been read. The reviewed material was used to identify recurring assessment formats and mark-scheme expectations. Practice questions added to the app are newly written, not copied from PMT.

- OCR A Biology Module 1 question paper and mark scheme: https://www.physicsandmathstutor.com/biology-revision/a-level-ocr-a/module-1/
  - Reviewed patterns: experiment planning, explicit independent/dependent/controlled variables, genetic variation, micropropagation, plant hormones, percentage comparison, sampling and data improvements.
- OCR A Chemistry “How Fast?” topic question paper and mark scheme: https://www.physicsandmathstutor.com/chemistry-revision/a-level-ocr-a/module-5/how-fast-video-solutions/
  - Reviewed patterns: rate equations from initial-rate data, units for k, concentration-time graphs, tangent gradients, Maxwell–Boltzmann distributions, Arrhenius plots and multi-part calculations.
- OCR A Chemistry “Acids, Bases and Buffers” topic question paper and mark scheme: https://www.physicsandmathstutor.com/chemistry-revision/a-level-ocr-a/module-5/acids-bases-and-buffers-video-solutions/
  - Reviewed patterns: pH/[H+] conversions, weak-acid approximations, Ka calculations, conjugate acid-base pairs and structured numerical working.
- OCR A Biology “Cell Structure” question paper and mark scheme: https://www.physicsandmathstutor.com/biology-revision/a-level-ocr-a/module-2/
  - Reviewed patterns: microscope calibration and unit conversion, biological drawings, identifying organelles from micrographs, and writing answers to the exact visual prompt.
- OCR A Chemistry “Aromatic Compounds” question paper and mark scheme: https://www.physicsandmathstutor.com/chemistry-revision/a-level-ocr-a/module-6/
  - Reviewed patterns: electrophile generation, mechanism steps, curly-arrow precision, reaction conditions and linked multi-mark explanations.
- Edexcel A-level Maths “Statistical Distributions” normal-distribution question paper and mark scheme: https://www.physicsandmathstutor.com/maths-revision/a-level-edexcel/statistical-distributions/
  - Reviewed patterns: inverse normal calculations, conditional probabilities, explicit hypotheses, p-values, contextual conclusions, and continuity corrections for normal approximations.
- Edexcel A-level Maths “Algebra and Functions” factor-theorem question paper and mark scheme: https://www.physicsandmathstutor.com/maths-revision/a-level-edexcel/algebra-and-functions/
  - Reviewed patterns: show-that substitutions, solving for constants, complete factorisation, linked parts, and method marks.

## Changes made

1. Added a new notes topic to each subject: Biology practical/data-response technique; Chemistry calculation, kinetics and practical-answer technique; Maths method marks, calculus and statistical interpretation.
2. Added 30 original questions (10 Biology, 10 Chemistry, 10 Maths) using the patterns above. New question cards identify their pattern source and are labelled as original practice.
3. Added a clear note that keyword auto-marking is a guide, not a replacement for human comparison with the model answer. Some valid paraphrases or alternative methods may not be recognised automatically.
4. Preserved the existing app and local-storage architecture.

## Important limitations

- This is an initial PMT-informed sample pass, not an exhaustive review of all available question sets or mark schemes. Continue topic by topic across the full specifications.
- PMT topic sets may include older questions; check the current OCR/Pearson specification before treating every detail as examinable.
- Auto-marking is still keyword-based. Calculation-heavy answers need careful self-checking against the displayed model answer; a fully reliable mathematical equivalence checker is not implemented.
- No claim of 90–100% verified specification coverage is made from this pass alone.
