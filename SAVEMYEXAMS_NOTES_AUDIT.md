## How these were used
The publicly visible pages expose board codes, topic trees and some topic summaries. These were used to choose supplementary note areas and cross-reference URLs. Additional explanations in the app are newly written concise revision content and are not represented as quotations or complete extracts from Save My Exams. Full notes/PDF access may be account- or subscription-gated.

## Added notes groups
- Biology: practical skills/data analysis; foundations/core biology; exchange, transport and immunity.
- Chemistry: practical chemistry/measurement; qualitative analysis/purification; equilibria/acids/organic mechanisms.
- Maths: pure methods; statistics and mechanics.

## Limitations
This update is not an exhaustive audit of every Save My Exams page, nor does it certify 90–100% specification coverage. Topic details and syllabus scope must be checked against the current OCR A H420, OCR A H432 and Pearson Edexcel 9MA0 specifications.
